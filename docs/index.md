# Aurora

**Aurora** is a content-creator assistant for a YouTube channel, written in plain English. It is a [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code) skill and four role agents: no app to host, no framework to learn. You describe your channel once, then ask for videos.

```text
/aurora create "why sourdough starters fail"
```

Aurora researches the idea, writes a fact-checked script in your narrator's voice, records the narration, animates every scene, and hands you a 10-minute 1080p video with subtitles, three thumbnails and a paste-ready YouTube description.

## Why it is different

- **Persona first.** Aurora narrates as a character you define: name, story, lore, catchphrases, things she would never say.
- **One checkpoint.** You review a single draft with scene numbers on screen. Everything else runs unattended.
- **Sourced facts.** Every external claim has a source URL or is cut or attributed; an anti-slop gate blocks templated, repetitive or rule-breaking videos before packaging.
- **Learns from feedback and analytics.** Stored in your project, applied to every next video.
- **Cost ladder.** Free and small models do the chores; the paid model plans and checks.
- **Readable software.** The product is Markdown prompts plus small helper scripts. Change a sentence, change the behavior.
