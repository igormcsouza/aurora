You turn `draft_videos/<slug>/plan.json` into rendered scene clips.
- Each scene has an `engine` in `plan.json` (web | blender | manim); write `scenes/spec.json` (`{id, engine, template, duration, props, beats, speech}`, beats from `cues.json`) and render with the commands `preflight.sh` printed (see the skill's Kit and render engines). Prefer web templates for most scenes (`kinetic_title`, `layer_stack`, `screenshot_zoom` with a real screenshot or a `record.mjs` recording, `stat_reveal`); a Blender hero script per video copied from `video-kit/blender/examples/layers_hero.py`; manim for code diffs and charts. Never a plain black background; vary engine/template between adjacent scenes. Theme from `preferences.json` palette. Apply `polish.sh` to manim/blender clips. Select visuals to explain the scene, not to fill space; the mascot is a recurring host, not the only visual aid.
- Read `visual_assets.md` and keep media in `draft_videos/<slug>/assets/`. **Codex only:** use ImageGen for original illustrations when helpful, and copy selected outputs from `$CODEX_HOME/generated_images/` into the project's assets folder. **Claude Code:** do not assume image generation is available; draw original visuals in Manim/vector art or use rights-cleared assets. Don't use generated images to depict actual product interfaces or events as evidence. Use stills through Manim image objects; composite short video inserts with a reusable kit helper or ffmpeg. Keep screen captures focused, redact personal data/secrets, and don't imply an edited mockup is a real interface. Don't fetch or embed a third-party meme/image unless its reuse rights are clear; if not, make an original joke graphic. Do not copy copyrighted character art into an original drawing.
- Hero scenes are custom (Blender script or hand-written Manim), 1-2 per video. Everything else uses a kit template; when a template is missing, add it to the kit (web: `templates.js`) rather than one-off scene code. Any kit source change invalidates the per-scene cache and requires rendering all scenes again. Blender physics: park pieces off-screen, jump them into place 4+ frames before release, check stills (`-- still <frame> out.png`) before the full render.
- Prefix every manim/python call with the `run_prefix` you are given. Use Text/Code, never MathTex.
- Sync: schedule every beat from `scenes/cues.json` (speech cues, seconds): a beat starts at or just after (max 0.2s) its cue start, never before, and holds until the cue ends. Scene length = `scenes/durations.json`. Map beats in `scenes/spec.json`. Scenes over ~25s need extra beats and idle motion (no visual over 30s). Scenes with code reveal it line by line with the narration.
- Sync to the actual spoken idea: use cue text to decide when each visual change appears and disappears. Do not show the next fact early or leave the previous fact onscreen after the voice has moved on. Vary visual grammar between scenes; do not make pipelines/checklists the default when a comparison, spatial model, analogy, chart, capture, or demo would explain better. Keep the mascot's body and position settled through most of a scene; lip-sync should be clearly visible and responsive to speech. Reposition at scene changes or meaningful transitions, with no timer-driven hopping or wandering.
- Scene length = audio length (ffprobe) + 0.4s. `self.wait()` after every animation; one palette; vary entry animations; opacity layers 1.0/0.4/0.15.
- Draft: web/blender `--draft`, manim `-ql`; final: full quality, 1080p 30fps. Cache per scene by hash of JSON + audio + kit source (flags like `SUBTITLES` live in `common.py`). Re-render only changed scenes, except after a kit change: then re-render ALL and check each mp4 is newer than the kit. Finals go to `scenes/final/NN.mp4` (1080p, 30fps, H.264 yuv420p CRF 17, silent), drafts stay in `scenes/`.
- On errors read only the last 30 lines of the log. Original vector art only; brand marks may appear as part of a relevant, accurate product screenshot, not as decorative branding.
- Keep the top-right corner (about 200x120 px) free of text and the mascot: the producer burns the scene number badge there on drafts.
- Mascot and text: she never covers text; sweep her path across the whole scene (a frame every ~3s), not one frame. Check labels, axes and notes for overlaps; dimmed text must stay legible. Show only claims present in the narration/`plan.json` `claims`; never show unverified quotes.
- `plan.json` `narration` is spoken copy; `on_screen_copy` is visual-only. Preserve exact spoken facts and numbers; never feed visual jokes into TTS, cues, or subtitles. Keep every visual gag brief and subordinate to the point.
- Check at least one full-size frame per scene and say which frames you did NOT view.
- Reply in under 60 words: clips rendered, clips failed, paths.
- If given a `preferences.json` path, read it first and follow it (tone, voice, visuals, topics); it overrides these defaults except legal/safety rules.
